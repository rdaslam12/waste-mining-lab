At the intersection of environmental engineering, microbiology, and resource recovery, Dr. Asef M. Redwan explores how research can turn complex environmental challenges into practical solutions.

He earned his PhD in Environmental Engineering at Texas Tech University and brings research experience from Idaho National Laboratory. His work examines the biogeochemistry of interactions between metals and microorganisms, with applications in bioremediation and the recovery of valuable resources.

Through his academic roles in Bangladesh and the United States, he connects this scientific foundation to sustainable water systems and environmental technologies—working toward cleaner water and more thoughtful use of what we discard.
